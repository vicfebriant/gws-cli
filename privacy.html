Privacy policy

gws CLI is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer, with their own Google accounts.

  • Google user data accessed through the Google APIs is used only to carry out the owner's own commands (for example listing, reading or editing their files).
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
